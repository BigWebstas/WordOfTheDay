Merriam-Webster's Word of the Day, with every word archived on your device so the whole history stays available offline.

<b>Today</b>
The newest word as a highlighted card above the archive, with a day streak. Pull to refresh. Tap a card for the full entry: meaning, usage, examples and "Did you know?".

<b>Rating</b>
Rate words from one to five stars, search them, and sort by top rated or newest. Swipe a word away to remove its rating, with undo.

<b>Quiz</b>
"Which word means…" questions, weighted towards the words you miss. Mark words as learned to stop seeing them.

<b>More</b>
• Home-screen widget with its own theme, size, opacity and tap action
• Daily reminder at a time you choose
• Share a word as text, Markdown or an image card
• Pronunciation audio on every card
• Follows the system dark or light theme, or override it
• Backup and restore of your ratings
• Optional Pebble watchapp: rate words from your wrist, with ratings synced to the phone

Words come from the public Merriam-Webster RSS feed. The app has no accounts, ads or tracking.
